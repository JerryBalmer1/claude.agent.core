── Retrospective — six parts, under twenty lines
 1. Gap: packet said / what happened / root cause, asked until the
    answer is a rule, a tool, or a fact about the repo. Never a person.
 2. Waste: steps that produced nothing, counted.
 3. One change: smallest edit removing the largest gap or waste.
    Proposal, not applied.
 4. Silent fixes: anything corrected but not reported. Expected: none.
 5. Shape: this run in a phrase you'd reuse.
 6. Recurrence: step sequences recognised from reports in any tree
    you can see. Count. No recommendation.
