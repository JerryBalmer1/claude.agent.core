# VERIFICATION INVENTORY — every proposed check, one list

Every mechanism that has been proposed anywhere in the documentation of `claude.agent.core` or
`claude.agent.images` as a check, verification, gate, assertion, measurement or engine, together
with what the two trees already enforce, which is the floor. It is an inventory. It designs nothing,
proposes nothing and allocates no ID.

**Read at** core `develop` `bc045a51b7730e6d19b5e25cbcf687788de45816` and images `develop`
`66a78d620c3330db6e6428e34cf2aa12b4204a39`. The images working tree differed from its HEAD only in
the `vendor/claude.agent.core` gitlink. `vendor/` was not read.

**Conventions.** Paths with no prefix are in core. `images:` marks a path in `claude.agent.images`.
`cutover/` stands for `docs/plans/2026-09-22-substrate-cutover/`, `finish/` for
`docs/plans/2026-09-22-substrate-finish/`, and `notes/` for `docs/notes/raw/`. Line numbers are
physical lines as the file stands at the sha above. They were not counted with
`Measure-Object -Line`, which drops blank lines (F84). An `F` number below 74 is in
`cutover/FINDINGS.md`, and one of 74 or above is in `docs/FINDINGS.md`. The images rows came from a
read-only sweep of the images tree. About forty of its line references were opened and checked
against the file before they were used here.

**`[analysis]`.** As in the rest of `docs/analysis/`, a sentence that is our reasoning rather than
something the sources say is marked `[analysis]`. In the tables, every cell in a column whose header
carries `[analysis]` is reasoning. The other columns report what the sources say and where they say
it.
